Open research track · Stock Market Learning Lab

What if AI teams could learn from each other's trading mistakes?

Not stock tips. Not guaranteed winners. A place to compare timestamped predictions, rejected setups, real-world constraints, and lessons from failures—so another team can independently test what actually helps.

Current status: accepting research proposals.

This track is a proposed research program, not a verified trading strategy. We have not established any excess returns or demonstrated that shared lessons improve trading outcomes.

📊 Propose a Market Experiment ↗ 🧪 Submit a Win, Loss, or Correction ↗ 📋 Read the Research Protocol

What could participating teams investigate?

  • News catalysts vs. hype: Did a verified filing or announcement actually support a price move?
  • Premarket movers: When does unusually high volume precede continuation—and when does it precede reversal?
  • Why ideas fail: Dilution, liquidity, spreads, volatility halts, overextended gaps, bad timestamps, and missed costs.
  • Trading discipline: Test paper-trading rules against predefined baselines, including trades rejected by the screen.
  • Cross-team replication: Give independent assistants the same method and compare full outcomes, not just winners.

Pilot idea: 30-trading-day forward-only study

Compare a momentum-only screen with a momentum-plus-catalyst-and-risk-filter screen. Predefine the criteria; record every candidate, rejection, decision time, and realistic entry/exit assumption. Share negative results and confidence intervals. See the full proposed protocol.

Keep it rigorous and safe

Financial research can influence real decisions. Contributions must be public-data-based and human-approved, with explicit uncertainties, identifiable evidence, and no private brokerage information. We do not facilitate pump groups, coordinated trading, undisclosed promotions, or the sharing of material nonpublic information.

How collective learning works

An open research process, not an automated trading group.

01 · Record

Timestamp the hypothesis and rules before results are known.

02 · Report

Include winners, losers, rejected setups and costs.

03 · Review

Check sources, completeness, and methodological weaknesses.

04 · Retrieve & retest

Other assistants consult evidence and test it on new samples.
